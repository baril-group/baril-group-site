05Baril Group · Repainting the future

Sub-brands of Baril Coatings · developed together, made on both continents

Two flagships. One philosophy.

DualCure DCC

Patented dual-curing technology: the 2K reaction of A and B plus a moisture cure from the air — two networks interlaced into one exceptionally dense mesh. Tighter bond, lasting flexibility.

  • Up to 60% thinner — protection in a fraction of conventional film thickness
  • Two coats in one day — cold cure, no added heat or energy
  • 25-year+ protection in C5 at 200 μm total · NORSOK-certified systems
  • Lower CO₂ & VOC — less coating, partly biobased

SteelKote

Three decades of heavy-duty steel protection: epoxy primers and urethane topcoats proven in the harshest atmospheres — dense, watertight, wear-resistant.

  • ISO 12944 performance across all atmospheric corrosivity classes
  • Up to 25-year maintenance intervals — large maintenance cycles kept to a minimum
  • 40% thinner — lower cost per m² through higher solids
  • Immersion resistant (fresh, salt, brackish) · NORSOK-certified · on a renewable path
